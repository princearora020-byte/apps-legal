PDF Toolkit — Privacy Policy

App: PDF Toolkit (com.prince.pdftoolkit) · Last updated: 12 November 2026

Summary

PDF Toolkit works entirely on your device. Your files are never uploaded to us or to anyone else. We do not create accounts, we do not ask for your name, email or phone number, and we do not track your activity.

Files you open

Documents and photos you choose are read and processed locally inside the app. They are never transmitted off the device. Processed files are saved only to your device's Downloads folder when you tap Download. We have no server that receives, stores or can access your files.

Permissions

The app requests no sensitive runtime permissions. Opening a document or taking a photo to scan is handled by your phone's own system file picker and camera app, which return only the item you selected.

Data we collect

We ourselves collect nothing: no accounts, no analytics, no crash reporting, no location, no contacts. The only data leaving your device comes from the Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) SDK described above, which may process your advertising identifier and basic device and usage information for ad delivery and measurement.

Local storage

The app keeps a small local history (file name, size and date of processed files) and your settings on your own device using local storage. This data never leaves the device and is removed when you clear it in the app or uninstall the app.

Advertising

This app shows advertising through Google AdMob to keep it free. AdMob may collect and use your device's advertising identifier, coarse device information and app interaction data to serve and measure ads. We do not receive your personal files or any content you process with the app.

The app asks for your consent before serving personalised ads. On the first launch you can accept or decline, and you can change your choice at any time by clearing the app's data. Where required, Google's consent management is used and non-personalised ads are served if you decline.

Learn how Google uses data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Children

The app is a general-purpose utility and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this same address with a new date.
