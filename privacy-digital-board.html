Privacy Policy — Digital Board

App package: com.prince.whiteboardonline · Last updated: 11 November 2026

Digital Board is a whiteboard and drawing app for writing, sketching, teaching and presenting. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

No account required

The app does not ask you to sign up, log in, or provide a name, email address or phone number. There is no user profile and no cloud account.

Your drawings stay on your device

Boards, pages and drawings you create are saved in the app's private local storage on your device. We have no copy of them and cannot view them. Uninstalling the app removes this data, and clearing the app's data in Android Settings removes it immediately.

Files you import and export

If you import an image or PDF into a board, or export a board as PNG or PDF, the app uses the Android file picker to read or save that one file that you choose. Nothing is uploaded anywhere, and the app does not browse or scan your gallery, storage or other apps.

Information we collect

We do not collect personal information. Specifically, the app does not collect or transmit:

  • your name, email address, phone number or contacts
  • your location (precise or approximate)
  • photos, files, messages, calendar, SMS or call logs
  • your device advertising ID or other persistent identifiers
  • your drawings, board contents or usage history

Analytics, ads and third parties

There is no advertising in the app, no advertising SDK, and no analytics or crash-reporting SDK that sends your data to us or to third parties. We do not sell or share personal data, because we do not collect any.

Permissions

The app does not request access to location, contacts, camera, microphone, SMS or your photo gallery. File access happens only through the system file picker for the specific file you choose to import or export.

Children

The app is suitable for general audiences and is used in classrooms. It collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date.
