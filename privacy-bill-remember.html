Privacy Policy — Bill Remember

App package: com.billkeep.reminder · Last updated: 11 November 2026

Bill Remember is a bill tracking and reminder app. It does not require an account, it works fully offline, and it does not upload your bills or anything else.

The app requests no internet permission at all. It has no server, no account system and no tracking. Nothing you enter ever leaves your phone.

Data you enter

Bill names, amounts, due dates, notes and payment history are stored only in the app's private local storage on your device. We have no copy of it and cannot read it. Uninstalling the app deletes this data, and Settings also has a "Delete all bills" option that removes everything immediately.

Reminders

Notifications and reminders are scheduled locally by your device using the Android alarm manager. No bill data is sent anywhere to deliver them, and reminders keep working with no connection.

Analytics, ads and trackers

The app collects no analytics, no advertising identifiers and no device identifiers. There is no advertising SDK and no third-party tracker of any kind.

Permissions

The app requests only these permissions: notifications (to show bill reminders), the ability to schedule exact alarms (to deliver reminders on time), and permission to restart reminders after your phone reboots. It does not request internet access, location, contacts, camera, microphone, files or storage access.

Data deletion

Because no personal data is stored on any server, there is nothing to export, correct or delete on our side. All data lives on your device and is removed when you uninstall the app or use the in-app delete option.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date.
